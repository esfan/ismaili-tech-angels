Who we are

Who we invest in

A highly selective process

We identify and support only the highest-potential teams and companies that already have proof points of real traction.

  1. 01

    Community

    A founder, executive or leader at the company is affiliated with the Ismaili community.

  2. 02

    Impact

    You're all in: high ambition, high credibility, and unique knowledge and skill in your market.

  3. 03

    Traction

    You have product-market fit, customers, revenue and a model that works.

  4. 04

    Funding

    We don't lead rounds. We join as strategic investors and advisors alongside your lead investors, and help you navigate funding terms, governance and economics as trusted inside partners.

How we help

What founders get

01

Mentorship

Specific advice from people who have done it, and sometimes uncomfortable. The liability clause your contract is missing. How to run your raise. What breaks at the next stage.

Our edge 02

Connections

Our networks are extensive. One introduction from someone with a board seat, or a company they built, can open a door that cold outreach never will.

03

Capital

Your primary capital comes from your lead investor. We add $25K to $100K in seed, Series A or Series B rounds and join you as your unfair advantage.

What founders don't get

We won't build your business for you, or hold your hand.

This is for founders who are already moving and want to move faster with trusted advice. We don't want board seats. We are builders and founders ourselves. We've seen the ups and downs, lived through it all, and we are here to support our community. This is our passion, not our main business.
